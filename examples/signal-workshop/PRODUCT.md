# Night Shift

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Players build systems that interpret messy equipment reports and make dispatch decisions.
Developers see live TypeSafe Jev judgments composed with Conjevture's typed probability models.

## Core experience

Keep two short gate lessons. Progress through six operational shifts with a limited
number of judgments, gates, drones, and inspections. Players edit natural-language
questions, connect gates, inspect probabilities, and see the consequences of their rules.

Each shift also asks players to investigate a failure in question meaning, wiring,
dependence, or evidence. Scoring uses independently authored outcomes, not Jev predictions.

## Design principles

- Let the player see the report beside the system that interprets it.
- Make question meaning, circuit logic, model assumptions, and action thresholds distinct.
- Label live predictions and recorded responses honestly.
- Preserve evidence provenance and show uncertainty before an action.
- Give keyboard and touch players complete connection controls.

## Visual direction

A player reads short reports at a desk or on a phone during an evening break.
Keep white work surfaces and blue controls from Signal Workshop. Use a dark report
panel to distinguish incoming language from the player's editable system.
Show decisions and outcomes through text, not color alone. Respect reduced motion.
