Build a system. Take the watch.

You operate a remote station. Three reports arrive each shift. You have two drones and two inspections.

  1. Define what each judgment should mean.
  2. Wire the supplied gates into the dispatch policy.
  3. Evaluate a report with live Jev or a recorded response.
  4. Inspect the probability and its model assumptions.
  5. Run the system. The thresholds choose dispatch, wait, or manual review.
  6. Score at least 9 points and solve the incident investigation to advance.

A correct decision earns 4 points. An unnecessary dispatch loses 3; a missed fault loses 5. Manual review earns 1. Each inspection costs 1 point on that report. Reviewing everything cannot pass a shift.

Jev interprets your questions. Conjevture evaluates the Boolean rule. Scenarios have independently authored outcomes; Jev never receives the answer key.

Recordings are actual Jev responses to the exact saved questions and report. Editing questions or trying your own text requires live evaluation, unless a matching recording exists.

On a phone, use the connection selectors below the board. On desktop, select an output dot and then an input dot. Drag a gate's heading to move it. Escape cancels a pending connection.
